# PastelPulse

Based on a google sheet I made 2 years ago

A soft pastel daily habit, mood, and routine correlation tracker. 

PastelPulse helps identify real lifestyle patterns—like how screen time influences mood, or finding your most productive days of the week—using clean visuals and statistical analysis.

## ✨ Features

- **Daily Quick Logging**: Track sleep, Khan Academy study time, phone screen time, mood, and custom daily habits.
- **Dynamic Goals**: Set and customize your own daily targets (e.g., `< 2 hrs` screen time, `> 8 hrs` sleep).
- **Normalized Multi-Line Charts**: Compare different metrics side-by-side on a 0–100% scale without weird unit mismatches.
- **Linear Regression & Correlation**: Desmos-style ordinary least squares ($y = mx + b$), Pearson's $r$, and prediction modeling.
- **Day-of-the-Week Insights**: View breakdowns by weekday to spot patterns (like Wednesday phone detox trends).
